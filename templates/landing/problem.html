The problem

  1. 01

    A session ends when its context fills, the terminal closes or you change machine.

  2. 02

    What it knew about the work is gone: the decisions, the rejected attempts, the next step.

  3. 03

    Your code repositories cannot hold it. Commits record changes to the code, not the work around them. And work often spans several repositories.

  4. 04

    So the next session rebuilds it from scrollback and guesswork.
